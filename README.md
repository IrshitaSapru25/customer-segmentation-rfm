# Customer Segmentation Analysis RFM 

## Project Overview
This project focuses on segmenting customers based on their purchasing behavior using Recency, Frequency, and Monetary (RFM) analysis. The objective is to identify distinct customer groups and understand their value and engagement patterns to support data-driven marketing and retention strategies.

---

## Tech Stack
- **Python** (Pandas)
- **Power BI**
- **Jupyter Notebook**
- **Data Source**: Online Retail Dataset (<a href="https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/352/online+retail">UCI</a>)

---

## Key Analysis & Insights
- Calculated Recency, Frequency, and Monetary metrics for each customer
- Converted raw RFM values into standardized scores for comparison
- Segmented customers into business-relevant groups such as Champions, Loyal Customers, At-Risk, and Hibernating
- Analyzed customer behavior patterns across segments to understand engagement and value differences

---

## Customer Segmentation
Customers were grouped into the following segments based on RFM scores:
- **Champions**: Highly active and high-value customers
- **Loyal Customers**: Frequent and consistent buyers
- **Potential Loyalists**: Recent customers with growth potential
- **At-Risk Customers**: Previously active customers showing declining engagement
- **Hibernating Customers**: Low-activity and low-value customers

---

## Dashboard Overview
The Power BI dashboard provides:
- Segment-wise customer distribution using a treemap
- Recency vs. Frequency scatter analysis to visualize customer behavior
- Revenue comparison across key customer segments
- Interactive filtering for segment-level exploration

---

## Business Insights
- A small group of high-value customers contributes a significant share of revenue
- At-risk customers represent an opportunity for targeted retention efforts
- Segmentation enables focused marketing strategies instead of uniform outreach

---

## Outcome
This project demonstrates how RFM-based customer segmentation can help businesses understand customer behavior, prioritize engagement efforts, and improve decision-making in marketing and customer relationship management.
